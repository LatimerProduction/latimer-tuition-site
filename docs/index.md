# Latimer Tuition: one-to-one online tuition for UK families

Latimer Tuition is a Sheffield-based tutoring agency offering one-to-one online subject tuition for UK families. Primary, KS3, GCSE, IGCSE, AS and A-level tuition is available, depending on the subject and individual tutor. All lessons are online; there is no walk-in teaching venue.

Families can browse tutor profiles and arrange a free introductory meeting before choosing paid lessons. Rates, experience and availability vary by tutor. Lessons are pay as you go, with no sign-up fee or compulsory package.

## Before choosing a tutor: questions worth answering

1. **What is the goal?** Name the qualification (for example GCSE or A-level), the exam board if known, and whether the aim is catching up, keeping pace or stretching ahead.
2. **Which topics feel shaky?** A recent marked test or school report gives a tutor a much clearer starting point than a general description.
3. **How often, and for how long?** Agree a realistic weekly pattern that fits around school and homework rather than adding pressure.
4. **Is the online set-up ready?** A laptop or tablet with a working camera and microphone, a quiet table and paper and pens to hand.
5. **How will progress be checked?** Ask the tutor what they will look at after a few lessons and how they will share next steps.

A free introductory meeting is a good moment to talk through these points and see whether the tutor is a comfortable fit for your child.
